---
title: Glossary
subhead: Every keyword the Arcs rulebook defines, in one place.
terms:
  - term: "Adjacent"
    slug: adjacent
    short: "Shares a thin border."
  - term: "Building"
    slug: building
    short: "Cities and starports."
  - term: "Bury"
    slug: bury
    short: "Place on the bottom of the Court deck."
  - term: "Capture"
    slug: capture
    short: "Place the prompted piece in the Captives box on your player board."
  - term: "Chapter"
    slug: chapter
    short: "The time in which players play rounds until they all run out of action cards."
  - term: "Clear"
    slug: clear
    short: "Return all pieces from the prompted area."
  - term: "Control"
    slug: control
    short: "You control a system and its contents if you have more fresh ships there than each Rival. On a tie, no one controls the system."
  - term: "Damaged"
    slug: damaged
    short: "Not fresh—a tipped ship or a building on its darker side."
  - term: "Destroy"
    slug: destroy
    short: "Return a fresh or damaged piece from the map. (It often goes to a Trophies box.)"
  - term: "Empty"
    slug: empty
    short: "Has no pieces in or on it."
  - term: "Fresh"
    slug: fresh
    short: "Not damaged—an upright ship or a building on its lighter side."
  - term: "Gain"
    slug: gain
    short: "Add the specified piece to your play area from its supply. (You can't gain it if its supply is empty.)"
  - term: "Give"
    slug: give
    short: "Add the specified piece to a Rival play area."
  - term: "Hit"
    slug: hit
    short: "Tip over a fresh piece on the map, or remove a damaged piece from the map."
  - term: "Loyal"
    slug: loyal
    short: "Of your player color."
  - term: "Out of Play"
    slug: out-of-play
    short: "Cannot be interacted with."
  - term: "Piece"
    slug: piece
    short: "Any component in the game."
  - term: "Place"
    slug: place
    short: "See Pieces & Piece Limits."
  - term: "Play Area"
    slug: play-area
    short: "On and around your player board. (Most pieces go in specific parts of your play area.)"
  - term: "Prelude"
    slug: prelude
    short: "See Prelude Actions."
  - term: "Replace, Return"
    slug: replace-return
    short: "See Pieces & Piece Limits."
  - term: "Rival"
    slug: rival
    short: "Of another player's color. When used alone, refers to another player."
  - term: "Round"
    slug: round
    short: "The time in which each player takes a turn."
  - term: "Scrap"
    slug: scrap
    short: "Make out of play permanently."
  - term: "Seize the Initiative"
    slug: seize-the-initiative
    short: "Take the initiative marker. You cannot do this if you have the initiative marker or if someone has already seized the initiative this round."
  - term: "Spend"
    slug: spend
    short: "When referring to a resource, return the spent resource to its supply."
  - term: "Steal"
    slug: steal
    short: "Add the specified piece to your play area from a Rival's play area. (This is distinct from take because some rules prevent stealing.)"
  - term: "Supply"
    slug: supply
    short: "The general supply holds resources and other shared pieces, and players' supplies hold their Loyal ships and agents."
  - term: "Swap"
    slug: swap
    short: "Give the prompted piece and take the prompted piece at the same time."
  - term: "Take"
    slug: take
    short: "Add the specified piece to your play area."
  - term: "Turn"
    slug: turn
    short: "The time in which a player plays an action card and may take actions."
  - term: "Turn Order"
    slug: turn-order
    short: "Starting with the player with initiative and going clockwise."
---

The rulebook defines these terms as it goes; this is the same list,
alphabetised, with a page of its own for each one.
